how to use this folder

Open any episode page to watch it: space plays, ←/→ skip 5 s, M mutes. Every playground runs in the browser with no install. Every model is plain numpy in ../code/, with backprop written by hand. To render an MP4: python src/make.py 9 --render. Your progress is saved in this browser only.
