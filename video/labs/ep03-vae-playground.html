Walk around inside a latent space

These are the real networks from the video: an autoencoder and a VAE, each squeezing MNIST digits through just 2 numbers. Everything runs in your browser.

1 · the latent map

Each small numeral is a test digit, placed at its code z. Drag anywhere, including the empty gaps, and the decoder draws what that point means.

What the decoder draws at the point you picked.

3 · draw a digit, watch it get encoded

Draw in the black box. The encoder maps your drawing to a point on the map (the chalk ring; for the VAE, the dashed ellipse is its cloud, ±2σ), and the decoder rebuilds it from those 2 numbers.

4 · interpolate

A straight line through latent space between two test digits, decoded at 10 steps. Try it with both models: the VAE's path stays on plausible digits.
