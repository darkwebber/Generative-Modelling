Guidance Lab: ask a generator for what you want

The real guidance models from the video run in your browser. Each is episode 10’s flow with one more input, the request y. Ask for a blob, a class or a digit, then turn the dial w: v = v∅ + w·(vy − v∅). Watch the samples obey, and watch what obedience costs.

what to look for

At w = 0 the request is ignored: every blob. At w = 1, plain p(x | y). Above 1 the points squeeze toward the blob’s most certain part. Pick something to avoid next door and the points slide away from it; push hard (w ≥ 2) and they overshoot.

In the video: at w = 1 a digit reader agreed with the request 86% of the time; at w = 1.5 97%, and FID was best; by w = 4 every digit was right, but the variety had collapsed.

The same models in Python: code/guidance.py, exported by code/export/export_ep11_assets.py. 2-D nets: [x (2), one-hot request with a “none” slot, sinusoidal embedding of t (64)] → 256 → 256 → 256 → 2, trained with the request hidden 10% of the time. MNIST: 784 + 11 → 1024 → 1024 → 784 with a clean-image head, warm-started from episode 10.
