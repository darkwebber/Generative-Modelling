Density Lab: fit the data, then dream

A generative model is a density p(x): a curve that says how likely every x is. Drag its bumps to fit the dots, and the log-likelihood score reacts instantly. Then let the machine fit it for you, and dream brand-new points from your curve.

drag an amber handle: left/right moves a bump, up/down makes it narrow and tall or wide and flat · click empty space to add a data point · shift-click (or long-press) a dot to remove it
dreams land here: x = μ + σ·z, with z ~ N(0, 1) and a bump picked at random by its weight
