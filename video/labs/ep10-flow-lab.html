The MNIST network: 2.7 million weights (8-bit here). It guesses the finished digit x̂, and the wind is v = (x̂ − x)/(1 − t).

1 step gives the average digit: a blur. By 8 steps the digits are readable; 32 look finished. Episode 9’s diffusion sampler used 1,000.

The same models in Python: code/flow_matching.py, exported by code/export/export_ep10_assets.py. 2-D nets: [x (2), sinusoidal embedding of t (64)] → 256 → 256 → 256 → 2, swish, t fed into every hidden layer, trained on straight roads xₜ = (1 − t)·z + t·x with target x − z; the reflowed net was trained on pairs (z, where round 1 sends z). MNIST net: 784 → 1024 → 1024 → 784 with an x̂ head.
