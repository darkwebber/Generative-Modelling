Score Lab: learn the arrows, then follow them

A real noise-conditional score network trains in your browser by denoising score matching. It sees a shaken point and the noise level σ, and predicts the noise; divided by σ, that is the score. Backprop is written by hand; no libraries are used. Look at its arrows at any noise level, then sample by following them, with plain or annealed Langevin.
