Energy Lab: sculpt a landscape, drop marbles

A real energy-based model trains in your browser. A small network gives every point an energy. Each step pushes the energy down under the data and up under the model's fantasies, which are marbles moved by Langevin dynamics (roll downhill, then jiggle). Backprop is written by hand; no libraries are used. Click anywhere to drop a handful of marbles there and watch where they go.

  • Set jiggle to 0, then drop marbles: plain gradient descent collapses every marble into a single point at the bottom.
  • 1 Langevin step per update: fantasies barely move and training goes wobbly.
  • α at its minimum: nothing stops the energies drifting off. Watch the chart.
  • Drop marbles on one blob and wait. How often do they climb over a ridge into the next valley?
The same algorithm in Python: code/ebm.py. E is a 2→48→48→1 network (swish activations). Fantasies live in a 2,048-point replay buffer; 5% restart from noise each update. Loss = mean E(data) − mean E(fantasy) + α·(E(data)² + E(fantasy)²). Adam, batch 128. The −log p shown is exact up to a grid: Z is summed over 64 × 64 points.
