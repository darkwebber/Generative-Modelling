Flow Lab: bend space, keep the sand

A real normalizing flow trains in your browser: a stack of affine coupling layers, each with a tiny neural network, all trained by exact maximum likelihood. Backprop is written by hand; no libraries are used. Watch the density sharpen, scrub through the layers, and click anywhere to see exactly how likely that point is.

Click any point to see its exact log-likelihood: log p(x) = log N(z) + Σ s
The same algorithm in Python: code/flow.py. Each layer is b′ = b·e^s(a) + t(a) with s, t from a 1→32→32→2 net (LeakyReLU); s is soft-clamped, s = 2·tanh(raw/2). Adam (β₁ = 0.9), batch 128.
