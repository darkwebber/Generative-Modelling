GAN Arena: a forger and a detective, live

A real GAN trains in your browser: two tiny neural networks, hand-written backprop, no libraries. Terracotta dots are the generator's fakes. The blue glow is the judge's opinion, where it thinks "real". White rings are the true data. Change the rules and watch what happens.

try this
  • Fast forger, slow judge (G lr high, D lr low): watch the fakes pile onto one blob, then hop. That's mode collapse.
  • 25 blobs: how many does it ever find?
  • Saturating loss + reset: early learning stalls. The judge wins too easily and the gradient vanishes.
  • Train until both curves approach ½: the judge can no longer tell.
The same algorithm in Python: code/gan.py. Networks: G 2→64→64→2, D 2→64→64→1, LeakyReLU, Adam (β₁ = 0.5).
